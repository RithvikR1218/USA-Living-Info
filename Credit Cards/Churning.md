# What:
Credit card churning is the process of getting credit cards for the [[Sign Up Bonus (SUB)]] and then canceling the credit card. 

# Why:
- The SUB is generally an incentive to get more people to get the card and is generally very attractive
- By using Churning, you can quickly travel credit/points which makes it much easier to travel and can save you hundreds of dollars in travel overall
