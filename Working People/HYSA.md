## What:
**High-yield savings accounts** (HYSAs) are specialized savings accounts that offer significantly **higher annual percentage yields** (APYs) than traditional savings accounts

* **Traditional Savings Account:** ~0.38% APY (national average) 
* **HYSA Average:** ~3.50% – 4.00%+ APY

# Why:
- Money in traditional savings accounts give you close to 0 interest rate. 
- While its fine to keep money there temporarily/monthly living expenses, Money saved there for long term will be losing buying power with inflation
- Keeping 6-12 month emergency savings in HYSA so that you don't lose out money to inflation while also keeping it liquid

> [!Note]
> - You might be asking why not use a CD. The problem with CD is that its not liquid and you can lose out on the gains if you have a random emergency like a health/car emergency
> - The HYSA allows for liquid money which can be used whenever it is required

## How:
Most HYSAs are offered by online-only, digital banks for financial platforms. Since these institutions don’t maintain physical branches, they operate with lower overhead costs

> [!Note]
> Some brick and mortar banks might have subsidiaries that can provide HYSA. 
> An example of this is Marcus by Goldman Sachs or the 360 plan by Capital One. Its upto you to decide which HYSA to choose from 

## Important Info
* **Variable Interest Rates:** HYSA interest rates are **variable** based on decisions from the Federal Reserve. When the Fed adjusts interest rates, your HYSA APY may fluctuate up or down over time.
* **Risk vs. Yield:** Some smaller or less-established financial institutions may advertise noticeably higher rates to attract customers. It is up to your personal risk tolerance whether to use them. **Always ensure the platform is FDIC-insured (or NCUA-insured for credit unions)** to protect your principal up to $250,000.
* **Checking + HYSA Setup:** Some people choose to skip traditional savings accounts altogether and use a **Checking Account + HYSA** combination. This is down to personal preference, but remember that transferring funds from an online HYSA to an outside checking account can still take **1–3 business days**.
