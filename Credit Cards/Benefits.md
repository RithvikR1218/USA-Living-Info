# What:
Seems kind obvious but the main reason why we use Credit Cards is for the reward points such as Miles and Cash back but there are other reasons why its better to use Credit Card assuming you are disciplined enough to pay them back

# Why:
- Having Credit Cards gives us a form of fraud prevention where we can cancel the transaction if it gets sent to a fraudulent transaction
- Its easier for the CC companies to detect these kinds of transactions before they actually happen
- Another reason is that its easiest way to build credit without having to take out some kind of loan
- It isn't worth buying a car on loan just for the credit score cause you have to pay interest but if you use your card properly, you dont have to pay interest 
- As for the main benefits, look at [[Miles vs Cash Back]] which will go into it in depth
