# What:
A credit card is a payment card you can use to make purchases online and in person. When you pay with one, you're authorizing the card issuer to cover the purchase on your behalf, with a promise to pay that amount back at a later date.

Beyond everyday purchases, many credit cards come with extra features such as rewards, introductory annual percentage rate (APR) promotions, travel perks and insurance protections.
