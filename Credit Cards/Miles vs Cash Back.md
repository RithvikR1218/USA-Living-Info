# What:
These are the main points/rewards that we get from the credit cards and honestly is the main thing you have to look at when choosing which credit card type you want to choose

# Cash Back:
- Cash back is much more obvious
- You pretty much get direct cash based on the reward points of the credit card
- An example would be lets say you have 3% cash back on groceries and you spend $100 on groceries, you make $3 from the cash back when you redeem your points
- Its the safe option and you can never go wrong with it either

# Miles:
- Miles are travel points that can be used to book Flights, hotels or get upgrades
- They are generally given out based on your flight booking which incentivizes more traveling but this normally happens for certain rewards model
- In general, you can assume 1 mile == 1 cent
- This means that if you have 75,000 miles from a sign up bonus, this is equal to $750 of travel credits
- The reason why you mainly use miles is because a lot of times, the airlines don't do a 1:1 when booking a flight ticket
- Lets say your flight ticket cost $1000, if it followed the 1:1, you would need 100,000 miles. Most airlines would ask for 83,000 Miles or we have a 1:1.2 conversion rate
- Some credit cards companies can have partners with different airlines and converting the credit card points to miles can be a 1:1.5 making it even cheaper when actually booking the tickets. Look at the transfer partners for the cards you are getting
- If multiple credit card companies have partnerships with different airlines, you can use both cards and get the best possible deals on that particular flight

# Why:
- There is no right or wrong answer if you want to choose Cash Back or Miles and it should be based on your lifestyle
- If you know that you aren't planning on traveling and would rather just have the money, go for the Cash back
- If you plan on travelling often and know that the miles will get used, you can go for cash back
- Just keep on mind for miles, its also to incentivize you to travel and you will automatically end up spending more which causes you to use the card more again
- This means that if you are trying to save money for something or don't want to spend as much, cash back becomes a better option
