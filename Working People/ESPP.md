# What
An Employee Stock Purchase Plan is an employer-sponsored benefit that allows eligible employees to purchase company stock at a discounted price using voluntary payroll deductions over time.

# Why
The main benefit for ESPPs are the **guaranteed purchase discount** (typically up to 15% off market value). Instead of holding it long term, you can sell the stocks immediately and pocket the discount. This ensures your portfolio isn't heavy on a single stock and you get a guaranteed returns as well

# How
- **Enrollment & Offering Period:** You select a fixed percentage of your post-tax paycheck (e.g., 1%–15%) to set aside. This money accumulates in an uninvested holding account during an **Offering Period** (usually 6 to 24 months). 
- **Purchase Date:** At designated intervals (typically every 6 months), the company automatically uses your accumulated payroll funds to purchase shares on your behalf at the discounted rate. 
- **Execution & Choice:** Once the shares hit your brokerage account on the **Purchase Date**, you own them outright and can decide whether to **sell immediately** (locking in the discount) or **hold** for potential long-term growth.

# Important Info
- The money is taken out pre-tax from your salary
- You cannot make lump-sum contributions during the purchase window—funds must be accumulated gradually through automated payroll deductions.

## Selling immediately vs. holding them :
- **Disqualifying Disposition** (Short-term Sale): If you sell shares within 2 years of the Offering Date or 1 year of the Purchase Date, the discount portion is taxed as **ordinary income** on your tax return. 
- **Qualifying Disposition** (Long-term Sale): If you hold the shares for at least 2 years from the Offering Date *and* 1 year from the Purchase Date, a portion of the gains may qualify for lower **long-term capital gains tax rates**.
