# What:
Sign up Bonus is a special one time bonus that the company gives you. You normally need to spend a certain amount in a certain time frame. 

>[!example:]
>- You get a SUB of $100 but you have to spend $300 in the span of 3 months to get it
>- The date starts from when you create the account and not when you receive the card

# Why:
- The company gives SUB as a way to get you to get the card
- Its a nice incentive but shouldn't be the main reason of why you get the card. The card's long-term benefits and everyday reward structure should match your actual lifestyle

# Additional Info:
- Depending on the card, they might give you miles instead of straight up cash but its generally just credit card points
- Another thing to note is that the rewards change over time and it might be worth trying to time your purchases to maximize the SUB
- Sometimes its $1000 for $4000 to spend but other times its $500 for $1000 spend
- Because minimum spending windows are tight (usually 90 days), it's smart to time your applications around naturally high-spending months (like upcoming travel, holiday shopping, or annual insurance payments) rather than forcing unnecessary purchases.
